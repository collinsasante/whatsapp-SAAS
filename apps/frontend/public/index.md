# Primary Services Offered by VerzChat

## 1. Shared Team Inbox

VerzChat's core product is a shared inbox where all customer messages from every connected channel arrive in one place. Every agent on the team can see open conversations, view full message history, and reply — all from the same dashboard, under the same business account.

The shared inbox eliminates the problems of one-agent-per-phone setups: missed messages, no coverage during breaks, no visibility for managers, and inconsistent replies. The entire team works together from one place.

---

## 2. Multi-Channel Support — WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram & TikTok

VerzChat connects to all five major business messaging platforms:

- **WhatsApp Business** — via the official Meta Business API
- **Instagram Direct Messages** — via Meta's Instagram Messaging API
- **Facebook Messenger** — via Meta's Messenger Platform
- **Telegram** — direct bot and channel integration
- **TikTok** — TikTok messaging integration

Every channel feeds into the same shared inbox. Agents do not need to switch between apps. Managers see all channels in one view.

---

## 3. Conversation Assignment & Management

Any agent or manager can assign a conversation to a specific team member with one click. The assigned agent is notified immediately. Unassigned conversations stay in the shared queue for anyone to pick up.

Managers can reassign or take over any conversation at any time. The universal takeover system means no conversation ever goes unresolved because the wrong person owns it.

Conversations can be labelled, resolved, and filtered. Full history is preserved for every contact across all channels.

---

## 4. Broadcast Messaging

VerzChat supports WhatsApp broadcast messaging, allowing teams to send bulk messages to their contact list for promotions, announcements, follow-ups, or re-engagement campaigns.

Broadcasts are sent through the official WhatsApp Business API — they arrive in the recipient's individual chat, not a group. Messages can be personalised with customer data. Delivery, open, and reply rates are tracked in the analytics dashboard.

---

## 5. Keyword Triggers & Automation Workflows

VerzChat supports keyword-based automation so that common customer questions are answered instantly — without requiring an agent to respond manually.

Businesses can define keywords (e.g., "price", "hours", "order") and set automatic replies. Automation workflows can also route conversations to the right team, send welcome messages on first contact, escalate unresolved chats after a set time, and trigger follow-ups based on conversation status.

This reduces agent workload and ensures customers always get a fast first response — even outside business hours.

---

## 6. Verz AI Assistant

Verz AI is VerzChat's built-in artificial intelligence reply assistant. It reads each conversation in context and suggests a relevant, on-brand reply that the agent can accept, edit, or ignore.

Verz AI is trained on the business's own knowledge base and conversation history. It never sends a reply automatically — agents stay in control. The assistant is designed to reduce the time it takes to draft a reply, especially for repetitive questions, without removing the human judgment that matters in customer communication.

---

## 7. Internal Agent Notes

Agents can leave internal notes on any conversation — visible only to the team, never to the customer. This allows agents to hand off context between shifts, flag urgent conversations for a manager, or document details without cluttering the customer-facing thread.

Internal notes improve team coordination and reduce the risk of dropped context when conversations change hands.

---

## 8. Contact Management

VerzChat maintains a unified contact record for every customer across all connected channels. Agents can view full conversation history, add contact details, and track the status of every customer relationship.

Contact management gives agents the context they need to reply personally and consistently — even if the customer has messaged on multiple channels at different times.

---

## 9. Real-Time Analytics & Reporting

VerzChat provides real-time analytics on conversation volume, response times, resolution rates, broadcast performance, and agent activity.

Managers can monitor team performance, identify bottlenecks, and make data-driven decisions about staffing, automation, and campaign strategy. Analytics are available directly in the dashboard with no additional tools required.
